# October 31–November 1, 2026: seven new branches

**Source review, not an independent re-run.** Seven repositories were missing from the census and curated wall. Existing [SweepAtlas](https://github.com/anttiluode/SweepAtlas), [TransformerX](https://github.com/anttiluode/TransformerX), and [Genealogy](https://github.com/anttiluode/Genealogy) were already inventoried. [Curated pass](../../data/passes/october-31-november-1-seven-arrivals.json) records the typed ancestry and distinctions.

## October 31

**[TheCombRedux](https://github.com/anttiluode/TheCombRedux)** makes a real state → learned workspace → copy/invert-operator computation in a JS XOR world. In its [Gate 0 ledger](https://github.com/anttiluode/TheCombRedux/blob/main/results/GATE0_2026-10-31.md) the intact system's **Brier .29062 is worse than cut-read .24998**; Bayes accuracy **81.93%** beats learned **55.86%**. The causal loop is an implementational result, *not* competitive AI or Anthropic J-space.

**[VHSImageStitch](https://github.com/anttiluode/VHSImageStitch)** packages moiré-tuned Sigh features and observed-pixel stitching as [Windows EXE and Python source](https://github.com/anttiluode/VHSImageStitch/blob/main/README.md). **M toggles the visual moiré view, but underlying frequency filtering remains active for matching**. 2× grid size doesn't guarantee real detail and 3-D parallax remains a weakness.

**[2729](https://github.com/anttiluode/2729)** explicitly couples the [WallMemoryLaw](https://github.com/anttiluode/WallMemoryLaw) cavity to the [ChainNeuronsWithPhaseSpike](https://github.com/anttiluode/ChainNeuronsWithPhaseSpike) phase-pair writers: stored D controls Robin spring strength. The [source results](https://github.com/anttiluode/2729/blob/main/README.md) show read-only pings, analog selected echoes (**r=.95**) and spatial spring response. But the held-out A→B/B→A order task **fails**, with no advantage over pooled springs. Neumann zero modes, clock constraints, cloned background, and unforced twins are major physical-model qualifications.

## November 1

**[Sihtiimagemodel6](https://github.com/anttiluode/Sihtiimagemodel6)** uses competing convolutional sparse atoms to restore fogged 32×32 crops. [Source gates](https://github.com/anttiluode/Sihtiimagemodel6/blob/main/README.md) report Sihti-fog MSE **.00183** (CSC) vs Wiener **.00247**, independent **.00223**, EPLL **.00200**, but edge-F and flicker superiority tests fail; early stopping contributes to the MSE gain. **purify^8 at dt=.25 is a band-stop**, not universal low-pass, prompting a true heat-fog replication.

**[CortexWaveguideLens](https://github.com/anttiluode/CortexWaveguideLens)** routes six Fourier phase channels using earlier noise context and explicitly phase-transports synthetic visual memory. In [Stage 2](https://github.com/anttiluode/CortexWaveguideLens/blob/main/results/TEMPORAL_STAGE2.md), reset-copy MSE **.0816** beats raw **.3819** and stale **.5123**, but **ties** classical motion-aligned averaging with matched resets. No general video or neural-waveguide result.

**[JanusMultiscalePhaseLensSol](https://github.com/anttiluode/JanusMultiscalePhaseLensSol)** tests frozen complex patch vocabulary (which **loses** denoising to simple filters and its phase-free control) and then real frequency-specific displacement phases. [Experiment 002](https://github.com/anttiluode/JanusMultiscalePhaseLensSol/blob/main/results/EXPERIMENT_002.md) shows **.100 vs .205 px** error in held-out synthetic shapes, reversing on unrelated high-frequency patterns (**.088 vs .041 px** phase correlation).

**[Sihtiimagemodel7](https://github.com/anttiluode/Sihtiimagemodel7)** inherits Model 6 CSC, adds phase-congruency vs energy gating, mismatched-world and transported-confidence memory controls. The [preregistration](https://github.com/anttiluode/Sihtiimagemodel7/blob/main/docs/PREREG.md) and implementation existed, **but no committed held-out results ledger** was visible. Validation pilots and a preregistration are not test evidence.
