## Story2

[Source](https://github.com/anttiluode/Story2/blob/main/README.md)

The Distance Between Houses: a complete fictional political thriller, 24 chapters plus an epilogue, with a static reader and local audiobook/illustrated-film production tools.

The repository contains the prose, chapter manifest, Piper narration pipeline and Film Lab with graphic, local Diffusers and supplied-image modes; whole-book batch rendering can resume and join chapter videos.

Published prose and production software do not establish that generated narration or a finished film is included. The political scenario is fiction; no documented code ancestry from Story is asserted.

## ZetaDreamer3D

[Source](https://github.com/anttiluode/ZetaDreamer3D/blob/main/README.md)

Walk inside a sampled trained complex image field Z(x,y,t), preserving the original 32 prime-frequency arms and three complex hidden layers of width 128.

The exporter stores RGB and complex field samples in standalone WebGL2 HTML. A 96-cubed default, narrower t slab and tetrahedral complex-zero filament extraction address coarse, foggy exports; an upgrader repairs existing HTML viewers.

The browser samples textures rather than executing the full MLP at each ray step. Old quantized volumes cannot recover discarded detail; synthetic example checkpoints are not the user’s trained images. Riemann-zero markers and visual structure do not establish zeta solutions or an AdS dual.

## ZetaDreamer3DV2

[Source](https://github.com/anttiluode/ZetaDreamer3DV2/blob/main/README.md)

Phase Observatory extends ZetaDreamer3D with nine views, including interference residue, phase current, a harmonic reference and learned-minus-harmonic discrepancy.

The full original complex network is retained. The reference solves Laplace’s equation between two sampled complex end planes with Neumann lateral walls, making the comparison assumptions explicit.

The harmonic field is a chosen reference, not ground-truth reconstruction. Image magnitudes do not identify phase, two planes do not identify the learned interior, and sampled filaments are not physical singularities or an AdS/CFT result.

## FridayLolSol

[Source](https://github.com/anttiluode/FridayLolSol/blob/main/README.md)

Residue Orchestra is a local browser instrument for nonlinear interactions among 2–12 audio tracks: pair products, delayed antisymmetric products, envelope imprints and triple products.

An embedded AudioWorklet, synthetic demo, pair-selection matrix, independent start/in-point controls and live recording turn waveform relationships into an immediately playable DSP tool.

These are designed time-domain transformations inspired by cross-terms, not inverse Wigner–Ville synthesis, stem separation or quantum music. Tempo matching is manual and recording is live browser-compressed audio.

## GhostWorld

[Source](https://github.com/anttiluode/GhostWorld/blob/main/README.md)

Offline blind-prediction lab: observe two marked moving objects, disconnect the sensor, extrapolate an internal state estimate and score predictions before observations return.

The documented deterministic core separates hidden World from Belief. Hold, linear and three constant-turn hypotheses can be compared; replay preserves predictions made at the time.

The predictor is conventional state estimation, not a trained neural model; trajectory spread is not calibrated uncertainty. Its writable eight-oscillator graph is a separate instrument and does not currently steer predictions or establish visual identity memory.

## SliderV3

[Source](https://github.com/anttiluode/SliderV3/blob/main/README.md)

Eight damped oscillators write 16 graph edges from measured wave exposure; AB and BA histories leave structural differences that a later non-writing probe can read after fast-state reset.

Source-reported five-seed blind decoding and learning-off/erase/fixed-propagation controls support partial synthetic order memory. A separate 16-mutable-scalar comparison measures wave recall against recurrent and trace baselines.

Dense ESN16 is stronger at short-to-medium suffix lengths; all models approach chance by 16 further writes. Scalar slots and readout count were matched, but transducers, fixed coefficients and computation were not. No practical, energy or capacity advantage is established; SliderV3 is distinct from the existing Slider3 repository.

## Friday

[Source](https://github.com/anttiluode/Friday/blob/main/README.md)

A 30-node magnetic graph has 15 modes dark at its five connecting nodes. Changing outer phases preserves this hidden subspace; opening an internal phase makes those modes observable.

The source gives an analytic dark-mode basis and numerical observability audit. Connecting-node rank rises from 15/30 to 30/30 after an internal phase offset, while the weakest observation singular value remains small for small offsets.

Full rank is not robust retrieval. Rational outer phases can match the golden-angle visibility result at interior sensors. Ideal complex-amplitude/unitary dynamics do not establish durable memory under dissipation, a write-retain-read device, or a new theorem about pi.

## Evidence and unresolved work

Friday reports 15/30 connecting-node-observable dimensions at zero internal offset, then 30/30 at offset 0.0001, with a smallest normalized singular value of only 0.00000725. This is an access and conditioning result under ideal complex-amplitude sensing.

SliderV3’s [resource-matched report](https://github.com/anttiluode/SliderV3/blob/main/RESOURCE_MATCHED_RESULTS.md) reports five-seed wave/ESN16 single-read accuracies of 70.4/100.0% after two common writes, 71.7/90.8% after four, 61.7/59.6% after eight and 52.9/50.4% after sixteen. Mutable storage slots and observation counts match; transducers and compute do not. Multi-bit capacity, noise robustness and an equal-transducer comparison remain open.

GhostWorld proposes a trained memory behind the same observation-only predictor interface; its current wave graph has no demonstrated forecasting benefit. Zeta’s harmonic reference fixes mathematical assumptions without establishing a unique interior. Story2’s production pipeline is not evidence of published generated audio/video.
