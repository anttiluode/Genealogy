Separate JS sheets: persistent log-odds context and a generic activator/recovery reaction–diffusion medium. During sensory blackout, seeded-coupled, seeded-uncoupled and sham arms share the same trained context. Six held-seed gates C1–C6 pass in 32/32 source-reported runs; the +50-tick readout distortion is 0.3351 excess MSE; front radius 22.36; by +160 excess relative to sham is 0.000105. **Boundary and impairment coupling are hand-specified**. Stored-state equality and decoupling are architectural invariants, not discovered physiology. This is neither a mechanistic migraine model nor a synthetic consciousness assay.

## Qwenimagination

[README](https://github.com/anttiluode/Qwenimagination/blob/f18c0bb952faa7c0f654f290ce8230005b13180b/README.md) · [Protocol](https://github.com/anttiluode/Qwenimagination/blob/main/PROTOCOL.md)

Proposed Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct test: hold a derived answer never written into either context's tokens, then recall it in a disjoint conversation via an external concept/drive loop. The protocol predeclares G0a/G0b checks, G1–G3 superiority tests, strict answer-versus-cue scoring, equal next-token KL damage budgets, and no-state / cue / oracle / in-context / snapshot / open versus closed-loop routes. **Nov 18 correction:** trained-Qwen runs now exist. V2 held-out derived concept transfer yields 18/60 versus 1/60 no-write and 0/60 cue/shuffled; V3 replicates 11/50 versus 1/50. Direct bag readers recover 29/60 and 25/50. V5 one-shot delayed writes give 0/232 exact, and V6 answer-token oracle 46/60 versus pre-question 2/60. V7 is inconclusive: even printed-word category queries fail due to the A answer bias; the external supervised classifier reaches 91.25%. These are not demonstrations of autonomous thought or persistent native memory.
