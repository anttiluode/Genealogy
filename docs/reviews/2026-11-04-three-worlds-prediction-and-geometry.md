## The actual forks of the question

- **SelfIndexingBeach — what can a reader discover of a changing medium?** The 36-link ring's sand state affects wave conductivity. Weak multi-query echoes drive an external ridge model predicting the echo *change after a known next write*. In one synthetic split the relative MSE is **0.000476** (read + event) versus **0.20497** (event only). At a specified local sensitivity threshold, one query exposes 17 directions and four queries expose 29; that is **not** an invariant physical state dimension or a Hankel rank. [README](https://github.com/anttiluode/SelfIndexingBeach/blob/main/README.md) · [experiment.py](https://github.com/anttiluode/SelfIndexingBeach/blob/main/experiment.py). There is no internal adaptive pinger yet, and the weak AB/BA echo difference is not a demonstrated noisy order decoder.
- **EmergenceNoSpace — can relationships support effective geometry without a supplied coordinate lattice?** Every physical node holds conserved resource and inherited rule parameters; a dynamic unembedded network transports resource, adapts conductance, closes local two-hop connections and, in the evolving arm, buds children. Its *picture* uses a force layout; the physics do not. In a **three-seed engineering smoke run** (120 nodes, 2,500 ticks) the mean edge count is approximately **240 fixed, 294 rewired, 356 inherited-growth**; the pumping imbalance falls from **1.337** to **0.701 / 0.626**, but the models are not edge-budget-matched. An apparent near-3 growth exponent arises even in the fixed random graph from only about two unsaturated radii; a degree-preserving shuffled graph can also display near-3 spectral exponent. **No emergent three-dimensional space or intelligence is demonstrated.** [README](https://github.com/anttiluode/EmergenceNoSpace/blob/main/README.md) · [smoke results](https://github.com/anttiluode/EmergenceNoSpace/blob/main/docs/smoke.md) · [geometry.js](https://github.com/anttiluode/EmergenceNoSpace/blob/main/geometry.js).
- **Emergence3D — what shapes does evolution produce when space is supplied?** The original [Emergence](https://github.com/anttiluode/Emergence) genome/translator and STATE-token feedback move onto a real **3D voxel grid**. Light arrives from above; nutrient rises from a floor reservoir, and cells shade one another. A single CPU seed in the README (24 × 64 × 64, 12,000 ticks) develops a living sheet at **70–78%** of the box height, with self-shading increasing captured light and some taller bodies; 419 founding clades fall to 12. This is expected ecological compensation-depth physics, **not** emergent geometry or verified root/canopy function. The GPU observations discussed elsewhere are not in this committed results ledger. [README](https://github.com/anttiluode/Emergence3D/blob/main/README.md) · [world.py](https://github.com/anttiluode/Emergence3D/blob/main/emergence3d/world.py).

## Why the branches belong on one wall, and why they cannot be collapsed

The conceptual ancestor is [ComputingBeachClaude](https://github.com/anttiluode/ComputingBeachClaude): a write may change the future read operator. SelfIndexingBeach isolates this question as an **observable Jacobian and external predictor**, not a physical-memory advantage result. Separately, [Emergence](https://github.com/anttiluode/Emergence) defines an inherited genotype-to-phenotype translator with environmental feedback. Emergence3D **inherits the code family** into a supplied 3D ecology. EmergenceNoSpace instead asks how interaction graphs and their operational distances change when there is *no coordinate grid*. Its relationship to Emergence and [EmergenceAstra](https://github.com/anttiluode/EmergenceAstra) is **inspiration, not an asserted fork**.

These projects are not one integrated AI. The wave-medium readout, coordinate-free graph and 3D ecosystem are three separate substrates with different observables and different update laws.

## Two decisive negative lessons

1. **Numerical resemblance is not dimension.** A random graph's finite ball-growth and return-probability slopes can resemble a 3D exponent when sampled over an inadequate scaling window. Require larger graphs, robust scale windows, graph ensembles, degree-preserving nulls, independent diffusion tests and response-propagation checks before claiming spatial dimension.
2. **Useful readout is not self-understanding or communication.** A ridge model outside the beach may decode its next response without the beach learning to read itself. Complex ecologies may produce persistent shapes without predicting held-out sun trajectories. Lower flow imbalance after increasing edges is not evidence of learned intelligent routing.

## The next experiment: one prediction that could separate the stories

Freeze simulator rules. In matched held-out worlds, compare the next observable state after a perturbation using (a) current measurements alone, (b) short histories, and (c) histories plus an interaction/message from a separate observer. Ask if communication improves **out-of-distribution** future prediction, not merely the similarity of two models' present representations. For NoSpace add equal-edge-budget graph nulls; for Emergence3D ablate sharing, shading and STATE feedback; for the beach include an internal active pinger, archive/no-archive and equal-probe-budget controls. There is not yet a shared causal-predictive performance result.
