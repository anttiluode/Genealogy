## 2026-09-15 — Clockfield pruning

The Clockfield family is best understood as theory inflation followed by unusually strong self-pruning. Nine repositories were added to the curated atlas: `ClockfieldBornRule`, `ClockfieldCollapse`, `ClockfieldBigBang`, `ClockfieldAsUniversalOperator`, `BirthOfClockfield`, `SimpsonsUniverse`, `HorizonNet`, `OutoSynapsi`, and `ClockfieldUnified`.

The important selection events are not the largest claims. `BirthOfClockfield` later corrects its own GUE/Riemann interpretation: the real-symmetric operator is GOE-like and the prime-trace test is null. `SimpsonsUniverse` contains a committed Straightening audit that kills the prime-specific ontology, the wrong GUE constant, α=1/137 construction, cyclotomic gauge-group story and other decorations while preserving a smaller Beurling generalized-number recursion driven by multiplicative lattice structure. `HorizonNet` independently turns a claimed inference speedup into a sound idleness diagnostic after discovering that competence lives in the transient rather than at the fixed point.

Two computational residues survive cleanly. `OutoSynapsi` shows that traffic can reshape an explicitly defined body geometry under resource constraints, while later gates show that different signal operators induce different useful geometries. `ClockfieldUnified` then extracts a Physics Router from the surrounding theory: persistent edge couplings plus local oscillator inertia/time scale route unseen spectral packets without a per-input branch selector, and local traffic eligibility plus one broadcast scalar consequence can train that body. The router explicitly makes no gravity claim.

The pass therefore keeps a strong distinction between executable toy dynamics, grand physical identifications, falsifiers/autopsies, and later computational extraction.

## 2026-09-15 — Graph navigation

The static atlas now carries its own SVG camera. Mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, dragging empty graph space pans, and `− / + / Fit` controls expose the same behavior without a mouse wheel. Zoom state survives ordinary graph rerenders such as selection and filtering, while a genuine change in graph world bounds re-fits automatically. The zoom code is dependency-free and receives the same CI syntax check as the main browser script.
