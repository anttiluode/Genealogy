# Research log

## 2026-09-15 — first archaeological scaffold

The census and genealogy are deliberately different objects: a repository can exist without receiving a lineage edge until there is evidence.

### Strong first-pass findings

- **PhaseStigmergy → FunctionalArbors** is explicit: the later arbor work reuses a PhaseStigmergy-style Laplacian/opportunity growth process while preserving the phase-specific negative result.
- **Operaattori → OperaattoriJako** is a clean extraction: a broad morphology-to-operator study produced a small reusable transport-vs-feedback diagnostic.
- **Geometric-Neuron → GeometricNeuronV24** is a correction lineage: V24 strips away the thermostat story and keeps the addressable-observation problem.
- **GeometricNeuronV24 + TinyAvatar2 → ReadWrite** joins sensing and intervention as `M J`.
- **LentoOrava** is the strongest practical extraction in the current AI/neuron spine because scalar intervention became PulseTriage rather than remaining only an analogy.
- **AnttisNeuron → GrowingAnttisNeuron → ActiveVectorNN → NewMachine → FusionMachine** is unusually explicit in the repositories themselves. The center shifts from modes/structure to developmental routing, resident-state/event separation, state repair/publication, then late relevance and routed writes.
- **Clockfield** belongs in the atlas partly because its direct adaptive-weight-decay experiment was negative. `ClockfieldMeetsGeometricNeuron` then turned a grand unification into operator, pinning and topology falsifiers.
- **Kompressori** is a model case of failure producing a better object: partial-field reconstruction mostly failed, revealing response geometry and low-rank operator updates.
