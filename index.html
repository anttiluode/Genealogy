Scientific timeline

SELECTION EVENTS

Corrections & dead claims

These are not embarrassing side notes. They are where the genealogy actually branches: an attractive explanation fails, and a smaller mechanism survives.

WHAT DID REALITY SAY?

Empirical evidence

Lineage confidence and scientific support are different objects. This view records experimental design, samples or seeds, controls, measured effects, replication status, limitations, and null or contradictory results without compressing them into one score.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND?

Unresolved questions

Evidence becomes useful for planning when it identifies what is still ambiguous. These records keep competing explanations alive, state the missing discriminator, and propose a concrete next experiment without pretending the outcome is already known.

USEFUL EVEN IF THE THEORY VANISHES

Tools & artifacts

This view cuts across genealogy families and research eras. It keeps the reviewed repositories that can be installed, played, measured or used directly, while preserving the same “what survived / what not to overclaim” ledger.

WHAT KEPT COMING BACK?

Survivor motifs

Mechanisms recurring across different metaphors—field, neuron, splat, GA, observer—are stronger clues than naming continuity.
